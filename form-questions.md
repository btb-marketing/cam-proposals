- What platform is your existing website built on? (WordPress, Shopify, Wix, etc.)

- Google Analytics: (radio) "I have access and I have added [analytics email] with full permissions" / "I have Google Analytics but I lost access" / "I'm not sure if I have Google Analytics"
- Google Search Console: (radio) "I have access and I have added [analytics email] with full permissions" / "I have Google Search Console but I lost access" / "I'm not sure if I have Google Search Console"
- Google My Business: (radio) "I have a Google My Business Profile but I lost access" / "I don't have a Google My Business Profile yet" / "I'm not sure if I have a Google My Business Profile"
- Do you have any big site changes planned within the next 12 months? (e.g. a content migration or site merger)

### Section 3: SEO Strategy
- Please list your top product categories that you want to target/improve
- Please list your top products that you want to target/improve
- Do you have any documents, research, audits, reports or any other resources relating to previous or existing SEO efforts?
- What keywords would you like to rank for? Please list in terms of priority and be as specific as possible.
- Who are your target audience? Have you identified different buyer personas?
- Who are your top direct competitors?
- Are you currently or have you previously done any PR (digital or otherwise) for your brand?
- Are you aware of your site ever having been negatively affected by any SEO core algorithm updates?
- Anything else you'd like us to know?
